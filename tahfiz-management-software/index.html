The workflow we show in a demo

  1. The teacher assigns the Hifz.
  2. The student practises on the web.
  3. AI screens the recitation. It checks words. It does not replace the Ustadh.
  4. The student submits the clip.
  5. The Ustadh reviews it.
  6. The Ustadh approves it, or asks for another recording.
  7. The parent sees the progress.

The student phone app is the Hifz desk and Academy. Institute, teacher, and guardian each have their own app. Guided Hafazan practice is on the web.
