The core loop

From assignment to parent visibility — in one flow

AI assists with routine screening. Your ustadh keeps the final decision. Families see the same truth.

  1. 01

    Assign

    Teacher sets surah & ayah portions by batch with due dates.

  2. 02

    Record

    Student practices with Virtual Ustadh and submits recitation audio.

  3. 03

    AI gate

    Transcript screening scores the attempt before it reaches the queue.

  4. 04

    Approve

    Teacher plays audio, reviews issues, approves or requests re-record.

  5. 05

    Parent sees

    Guardians get practice status, weekly AI report, and term progress.
