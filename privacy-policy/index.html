Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ilming (“we”, “our”) operates the ilming.io website and the ilming LMS application used by madrassas, Islamic schools, learning institutes, their staff, students, and guardians.

Information we collect

Institute administrators provide institute and user data (names, emails, phone numbers, exam results, fee records). Students and guardians use the app under their institute’s account. Technical logs (IP, device, audit events) support security and support.

How we use data

  • Deliver LMS features (exams, fees, notifications, certificates)
  • Process payments via Razorpay on behalf of your institute
  • Send service emails and in-app notifications configured by the institute
  • Improve security, prevent abuse, and comply with law

Sharing

We use subprocessors (cloud hosting, email, payment providers) under contracts. We do not sell personal data. Institutes control what guardians and students see within their tenant.

Retention & rights

Retention follows institute agreements and applicable law. Contact contact@ilming.io for access, correction, or deletion requests tied to your institute account.
