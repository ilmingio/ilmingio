Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

ilming (“we”, “our”) operates the ilming.io website and the ilming platform used by Tahfiz institutes, madrassas, Islamic schools, their staff, students, and guardians. The institute decides why student and guardian data is collected. ilming processes that data to provide the service. A signed data-processing addendum is available on request from info@ilming.io.

Information we collect

  • Account data: names, emails, phone numbers, roles, and institute membership
  • Education records: batches, attendance, assignments, exam results, certificates, and Hifz progress
  • Guardian links and the progress the institute chooses to show a parent
  • Fee records and payment references. Card and bank details are collected by the payment provider, not stored as full card numbers by ilming
  • Recitation audio, described below
  • Technical logs: IP address, device, and security events, including exam integrity events when an institute turns those checks on

Voice and recitation audio

When a student practises or submits a recitation, the recording is stored as a file on the application server. It is served through an access-controlled address. It is not placed in a public bucket.

  • Who can open it. The student, teachers reviewing that attempt, institute staff with access to the desk, and guardians when the institute’s guardian view includes approved recordings.
  • Scoring. When practice scoring is on, the audio file is sent to OpenAI (Whisper) to transcribe words for that attempt. ilming does not train its own models on these recordings.
  • Talking face. If the institute turns on the web talking-face add-on, the session uses D-ID. The private D-ID key stays on the ilming server.
  • Staff AI. Optional exam and study assistants can use an OpenAI key the institute connects itself. That traffic goes to OpenAI under the institute’s key.
  • Practice and recordings waiting for review. The student can delete these. The audio file is removed from the application server at that moment, unless another attempt still uses the same file.
  • Approved recordings. These stay for the life of the institute account, so the teacher and guardian record remains. They are not deleted on a timer.
  • When an institute leaves. Email info@ilming.io to request an export and deletion. Tenant recordings and records are removed when that request is completed. A database backup is taken daily and the local copy is deleted after it uploads. An older backup can still contain a recording until that backup is no longer kept for recovery.

Scoring is a word check. It is not a Tajweed certificate. The teacher makes the academic decision.

Exams and cameras

Default exams do not open a camera. If an institute requires a webcam, the student’s browser opens the camera for that exam to check that a camera stays connected. That video is not uploaded as a recording and is not used for face recognition. A disconnect is logged for staff. The exam form includes a screen-recording option. The current exam monitor does not capture or upload the screen. Checks that do run, when the institute enables them, are fullscreen, tab and window focus, copy and paste, and right-click.

Children

Students may be children. The institute is responsible for having a lawful basis to add them and to contact their guardians. Guardians have their own accounts. ilming does not sell student data and does not use it for advertising.

How we use data

  • Run Hifz, practice, exams, fees, notifications, and certificates
  • Take fee payments through Razorpay, Stripe, GoCardless, or ToyyibPay when that method is enabled for the institute
  • Send service email through our mail provider, and push notifications through Firebase when the institute uses them
  • Keep the service secure and meet legal duties

Sharing

We use the subprocessors listed on the subprocessors page. We do not sell personal data. Each institute is a separate tenant. Staff see the students their role allows.

Where data is stored

The application runs on AWS. Institute records are in MongoDB Atlas. Database backups run on a schedule. Recitation files stay on the application server. Some Quran reference clips are stored in Amazon S3. More detail is on the security page.

Retention and rights

People can download a copy of their personal data from Settings → Your data rights, or email info@ilming.io for access, correction, or deletion. Students and guardians can close their own account from Settings. Staff accounts are closed by the institute. Requests are handled under the law that applies to that person, including UK GDPR where it applies.
