Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 30 September 2026

These are the public terms for institutes using ilming.io and the ilming platform. By registering an institute, you agree to them for that organisation. A signed order can replace these terms. Enterprise customers can ask for that agreement from contact@ilming.io.

The service

ilming is a multi-tenant platform for Tahfiz institutes, madrassas, and Islamic schools. Hifz and Academy run in the student app. Guided Hafazan practice runs on the web. Features, student limits, and practice-scoring allowances depend on the plan on the pricing pages. A 14-day pilot is for evaluation. It becomes a paid subscription only when the institute starts one.

Subscription, cancellation, and refunds

  • The price is the one published for the institute’s currency. Annual prices on those pages include two months.
  • Paid plans renew until the institute cancels.
  • Cancel by emailing support@ilming.io before the next renewal. Access continues until the end of the period already paid.
  • A period that has already started is not refunded, except where the law requires a refund or we agree one in writing.
  • Practice scoring and the talking-face add-on are billed as described on the AI and pricing pages. They are not included in the free Halaqa plan.
  • Fee collection from parents is separate from the ilming subscription. Parent payment disputes stay with the institute.

Institute data

Student, guardian, academic, fee, and recitation records belong to the institute. ilming may host and process them only to provide the service, keep it secure, and meet the law. The institute keeps a licence to export that data. Email support to request an export or deletion when the institute leaves. Personal export and account closure for a student or guardian are described in the privacy policy.

Children, guardians, and recordings

The institute must have authority to enrol students, including children, and to hold guardian contact details. Recitation audio is stored and may be sent to the providers named in the privacy policy for word transcription or the talking-face add-on. ilming does not train its own models on those recordings. AI scoring is a word check. It is not a Tajweed ruling and it does not replace the teacher.

Institute responsibilities

  • Accurate registration data and a lawful basis for student and guardian data
  • Staff access, passwords, and any OpenAI key the institute connects
  • Exam settings that match the institute’s own policy, including webcam presence if it is turned on
  • Subscription fees and the payment-provider charges for the method the institute uses
  • Academic decisions, certificates, and compliance with local education rules

Acceptable use and suspension

Do not attempt unauthorised access, distribute malware, scrape the service at scale, or misuse exam integrity tools. We may suspend an institute for non-payment, a security risk, or a breach of these terms. We will give notice when that is practical. Suspension does not by itself delete the institute’s data.

Availability, support, and security

We do not publish an uptime percentage. We keep the service available with commercially reasonable care and take database backups on a schedule. Support for existing institutes is support@ilming.io. A signed order can add a support commitment. Security practices are described on the security page. No online service can promise that it will never be disrupted.

Third-party services

Payments, email, hosting, push notifications, transcription, and the talking face use the providers on the subprocessors page. Live class links the institute pastes (for example Google Meet or Zoom) are the institute’s own meetings.

Intellectual property

ilming owns the software, site, and branding. The institute owns the content it uploads, including lesson material and recordings of its students, subject to the rights of the people in those recordings. Quran reference audio used in practice is provided for that practice and is not a transfer of the reciter’s rights to the institute.

Confidentiality

Each side will keep the other’s non-public business information confidential and use it only to perform these terms. Student and guardian personal data is handled under the privacy policy, not as ordinary business chatter.

Liability

The service is provided with commercially reasonable care. ilming is not liable for the institute’s academic judgements, parent fee disputes, or a failure to follow local education law. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow a contract to limit.

Law

These public terms do not name a court, a governing law, or a registered company number. A signed order is where those are set, including for institutes in India, the UK, the UAE, and the GCC. Write to support@ilming.io to put governing law and jurisdiction in that order before you rely on these terms as a contract, and before starting a formal claim.
