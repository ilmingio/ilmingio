Pricing FAQ

Trials, billing regions, and what is included in each plan.

Halaqa is free for up to 30 students and does not expire. A 30-day pilot is for trying a larger plan on your own batches. Contact us to provision your institute admin on app.ilming.io. The public register page is for student enrollment, not institute self-signup.

Enterprise plans bill in GBP (UK), AED or SAR (UAE/GCC), with white-label subdomain and dedicated onboarding. Contact sales for a formal quote.

ilming subscription fees are separate from gateway charges. Razorpay, Stripe, GoCardless, and ToyyibPay (FPX) transaction fees apply at their standard rates when students or guardians pay through your connected account. UK and GCC enterprise plans can be invoiced in GBP, AED, or SAR.
