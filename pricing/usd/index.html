USD plan FAQ

AI usage, fair use, and how this differs from India INR pricing.

Each student recording is transcribed with OpenAI Whisper ($0.006 per audio minute). A typical sabk is about 40 seconds, four times a school day. That cost cannot sit inside a low LMS-only fee. The bundle still looks like one monthly number; the split is shown so institutes know what they are paying for.
