01

How Hifz actually runs

Teacher assigns. Student records. AI screens. Ustadh decides. Parent sees.

One loop. No WhatsApp audio dump. Approval never leaves the teacher.

  1. 01 Assign

    Portion

    Teacher sets surah & ayah by batch, with a due date.

  2. 02 Record

    Practice

    Student opens Virtual Ustadh, listens, records on the phone.

  3. 03 AI gate

    Screen

    Transcript screening scores the attempt before the queue.

  4. 04 Approve

    Ustadh

    Play audio, read the summary, approve or ask a re-record.

  5. 05 Parent

    Sees

    Weekly AI report, term progress, playable approved recitations.
