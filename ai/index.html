Hero AI feature

Virtual Ustadh — AI-guided Quran practice

Students record on mobile. AI runs transcript screening and coaching cues (Madd meter, score ring). Attempts that pass the gate wait for teacher approve or re-record — parents see the outcome.

  • Assign → Record → AI gate → Teacher approve → Parent sees
  • Live waveform, Madd meter, and score ring during practice
  • Weekly AI report and term progress for guardians
  • Acoustic Tajweed (Makharij / Madd / Waqf) on the roadmap

Responsible AI for Islamic education

We built ilming AI with madrassa and school realities in mind — not as a shortcut around teaching, but as a prep tool for educators.

  • Teachers and admins approve all AI-generated exam content before publish
  • Institutes control whether AI features are enabled at all
  • Student assistants are scoped to your uploaded materials — not open web search
  • Arabic and English supported; content must align with your institute's curriculum
  • AI is optional beta — core LMS works fully without it

How to enable AI

Available on Professional and Enterprise plans. Your institute admin connects an OpenAI API key in settings — usage and costs are under your control.

  1. Request a pilot or upgrade to Professional
  2. Admin adds API key in institute settings
  3. Enable features per role (staff vs student)
  4. Train teachers on review-before-publish workflow

Also live without AI

Secure exams, fees, families, live classes, certificates, attendance, and 10 languages — all production-ready today. Recitation screening is live; staff AI assistants are optional beta.
