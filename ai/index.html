AI assists. The Ustadh decides.

Students practise on the web until the ayah is ready. One recording goes to the teacher. This is a word check, not a Tajweed certificate. Scoring and the talking face are billed apart from the LMS. The phone app is Hifz and Academy.

Web practice

AI Hafazan Teacher

The teacher greets the student by name, plays a verified recitation, listens, and marks the missed word. The student repeats that word, then the whole ayah. Practice retries do not enter the teacher queue. The human Ustadh or Ustazah assesses the recording the student sends at the end.

  • Assign, practise on the web, send one recording, teacher assesses, parent sees the outcome
  • Word check only. Tajweed judgement stays with the teacher.
  • Quran audio is verified. English coaching uses one voice. The talking face is a web add-on.
  • Boys can use Ustadh Ahmad on the web. Girls stay on the portrait until the Ustazah video is ready.
How it is sold

Three lines, not one unlimited AI plan

Practice audio and a live face both cost money every time a student uses them. They are not folded into the lowest LMS fee.

Platform

Hifz desk, attendance, fees, exams, guardian view, and the teacher review queue. The free Halaqa plan is this line. Students can listen and repeat. Scored practice is not included.

Practice scoring

Starts on the paid plan. Circle and Basic include 20 scored clips per student each month. International packs from the starter price include 80. A plan that cannot cover the audio bill has scoring quoted separately. When that month’s clips are used, scoring stops. Students can still listen and repeat. Extra clips are not billed automatically. Practice is on the web.

Talking face

Web video of Ustadh Ahmad, billed as an add-on for the time the session stays open. Piloted one class at a time. It is not included in the free Halaqa plan.

Responsible AI for Islamic education

We built ilming AI with madrassa and school realities in mind — not as a shortcut around teaching, but as a prep tool for educators.

  • Teachers and admins approve all AI-generated exam content before publish
  • Institutes control whether AI features are enabled at all
  • Student assistants are scoped to your uploaded materials — not open web search
  • Arabic and English supported; content must align with your institute's curriculum
  • AI is optional beta — core LMS works fully without it

How to enable AI

Available on Professional and Enterprise plans. Your institute admin connects an OpenAI API key in settings — usage and costs are under your control.

  1. Request a pilot or upgrade to Professional
  2. Admin adds API key in institute settings
  3. Enable features per role (staff vs student)
  4. Train teachers on review-before-publish workflow

Secure exams, fees, families, live classes, certificates, attendance, and 10 languages — all production-ready today. Recitation screening is live; staff AI assistants are optional beta.
