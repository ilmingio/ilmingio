Advancing Islamic education through technology

ilming is an Islamic LMS built to simplify institute operations and elevate the quality of learning.

Islamic educational institutes deserve software as thoughtful as the education they deliver. ilming brings Hifz tracking, Virtual Ustadh AI practice, madrassa management, secure exams, fee collection, live classes, and guardian communication into one platform — so your team spends less time on admin and more time on teaching.

Our vision

To become one of the most advanced Islamic LMS platforms globally — making high-quality Quran and Islamic education accessible, affordable, and operationally excellent for Tahfiz institutes, madrassas, and schools in the UK, UAE, India, and beyond.

Our purpose

Simplify operations for educational institutes and provide high-quality learning tools that help madrassas, schools, and learning centres run with clarity, accountability, and care.

What ilming is — and is not

ilming.io is a software / SaaS education platform. We are not a stationery brand, e-commerce store, or consumer tuition marketplace. If you are looking for physical planners or journals, that is a separate business — ilming LMS lives exclusively at ilming.io and app.ilming.io.

We are also not competing with solo-creator course hosts or self-hosted open LMS platforms. ilming sits specifically for institutes that run Hifz circles, batches, exams, fees, and parent communication — Tahfiz institutes, madrassas, Islamic schools, and learning centres.

Regions we serve

ilming supports institutes operating in the United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, India, and the wider GCC, with regional billing (GBP, AED/SAR, INR), Arabic RTL support, and white-label branding for enterprise deployments.
