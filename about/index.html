What ilming is — and is not

ilming.io is a software / SaaS education platform. We are not a stationery brand, e-commerce store, or consumer tuition marketplace. If you are looking for physical planners or journals, that is a separate business — ilming LMS lives exclusively at ilming.io and app.ilming.io.

We are also not competing with solo-creator course hosts or self-hosted open LMS platforms. ilming sits specifically for institutes that run Hifz circles, batches, exams, fees, and parent communication — Tahfiz institutes, madrassas, Islamic schools, and learning centres.

Who operates ilming

Kiran Raj R is the founder and CTO. He builds and runs ilming.io and app.ilming.io. Institutes trust the product with children, guardians, payments, recitation recordings, and academic records. Religious judgement on recitation stays with the institute’s own teachers.

Questions and support: info@ilming.io. Official profiles: Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, and YouTube. How data is handled is on the privacy policy, security, and subprocessors pages.

ilming exists because many Tahfiz institutes still run memorisation on paper, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp audio, while parents have no shared view of progress. The product is the workflow assign → practise → AI screen → Ustadh review → approve → parent progress, with attendance, exams, and fees behind that.

Regions we serve

ilming supports institutes operating in the United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, India, and the wider GCC, with regional billing (GBP, AED/SAR, INR), Arabic RTL support, and white-label branding for enterprise deployments.
