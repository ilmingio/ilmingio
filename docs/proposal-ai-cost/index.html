How to price Quran AI on a proposal

Read this before you send a one-time quote. Whisper is billed every time a student records. A 500-student institute can cost us more in OpenAI fees than the whole one-time payment.

Do not do this

Do not sell unlimited Quran AI inside a one-time payment (setup fee, lifetime licence, “pay once”). After that money is spent, every new recording is still billed to our OpenAI account. With 500+ students that bill does not stop.

Do this instead

One-time fee = software setup + LMS licence for a defined period (or annual LMS). Quran AI = monthly or annual usage fee with a recitation cap. If they want a prepaid pack, it must run out when credits finish.

1. What we actually pay OpenAI

When a student taps Record & submit, ilming sends the audio to OpenAI Whisper (whisper-1). Whisper charges $0.006 per minute of audio. A short GPT message for coaching is extra but tiny (~$0.0002). Scoring the Arabic text is done on our server and costs $0.

Typical Hifz practice: 40-second clip, 4 times per school day, 22 days a month ≈ 59 minutes of audio per student per month.

Our cost ≈ $0.38 per active reciting student per month (typical). Heavy retry days ≈ $0.82. If someone holds the mic to the 2-minute cap many times a day, it can pass $2 per student per month.

3. Why a one-time fee cannot cover 500+ AI students

Example: we take $5,000 one-time and promise Quran AI forever for 500 students.

Usage We pay OpenAI $5,000 lasts about After that
Typical $188 / month 26 months We lose money every month forever
Heavy practice $410 / month 12 months Same — unlimited AI is open-ended
Retry / long clips $1,077 / month under 5 months The one-time fee is gone in one term

If the one-time quote was $2,000 all-in, typical 500-student AI usage burns it in ~10 months. That is why “pay once, AI included” is not affordable for large institutes.

5. Copy-paste for the proposal

Quran AI usage. Student recitations are transcribed with OpenAI Whisper ($0.006 per audio minute). This is billed as a recurring usage fee, not as part of the one-time implementation. For up to 500 active reciting students: $449 / month (or $0.90 / student / month above 500), including 80 scored recitations per student per month. Additional recitations: $0.02 each. Unused LMS setup fees do not include unlimited AI practice.

Never write: “Lifetime AI”, “unlimited practice included in the one-time fee”, “AI free forever after setup”.

6. If they insist on paying once for AI

Only offer a prepaid credit pack with a written cap, for example:

  • $1,000 credits ≈ 3–4 months of typical use for 150 students — then they top up.
  • $3,000 credits ≈ 16 months typical for 150 students, or ~4 months typical for 500 students.
  • When credits hit zero, recording still saves, but AI scoring pauses until they recharge.

Do not call that “lifetime”. Put the remaining-credit rule in the SOW.
