NIOS Date Sheet 2025 Released for Class 10 & 12 October–November Exams

The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) has released the official NIOS Date Sheet 2025 for Class 10 and Class 12 October session exams. Students preparing for the October–November public exams can now check their subject-wise schedules and download the PDF directly from the official website - nios.ac.in

The theory exams will be held from October 14 to November 18, 2025, across NIOS exam centres in India and abroad. Meanwhile, the practical exams for both Class 10 and Class 12 were already conducted in September 2025.

This article will give you all the important details about the NIOS exam schedule, sample subject-wise dates, how to download the date sheet and admit card, and preparation tips to score better.

Highlights of NIOS Exam Date 2025

  • Theory Exams (Class 10 & 12): October 14 – November 18, 2025
  • Practical Exams (Class 10 & 12): September 2025
  • Official Website for Date Sheet: nios.ac.in
  • Admit Card Portal: sdmis.nios.ac.in

How to Download NIOS Date Sheet 2025 PDF?

Students can easily download the official NIOS Date Sheet PDF 2025 by following these steps:

  1. Visit the NIOS official website: nios.ac.in
  2. On the homepage, check the “Latest Notifications” section.
  3. Click on the link titled “Date Sheet for Secondary (Class 10) / Sr. Secondary (Class 12) Examinations, October 2025.”
  4. The PDF will open in a new tab. Download and save it.
  5. Keep a printout or digital copy for reference.

How to Download NIOS Admit Card 2025?

Admit cards (hall tickets) for Class 10 and Class 12 October exams are available on the NIOS student portal. How you can download them:

  1. Visit: sdmis.nios.ac.in/search/hall-ticket. 
  2. Enter your 12-digit enrollment number.
  3. Select whether you want the Theory or Practical admit card.
  4. Click Submit.
  5. Download the PDF and take a printout (recommended for exam hall entry).

Important Notes:

  • Admit card is available only if exam fees are paid and your photo is uploaded.
  • Students must carry a printed admit card + valid photo ID to every exam.
  • If not generated, contact your NIOS Regional Centre immediately.

Preparation Tips for NIOS 10th & 12th Exams 2025

  • Follow the date sheet: Make a timetable based on your subject exam dates.
  • Revise smartly: Focus on important chapters, previous year question papers, and model papers.
  • Balance theory & practical: Don’t neglect practical exam preparation.
  • Stay healthy: Maintain proper sleep and avoid last-minute stress.
