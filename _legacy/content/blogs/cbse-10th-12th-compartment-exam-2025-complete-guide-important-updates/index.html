CBSE 10th & 12th Compartment Exam 2025: Complete Guide & Important Updates

  • Feb. 11, 2025, 5:30 a.m.

Overview of Class 10th & 12th CBSE Compartment Exam 2025

These compartment exams are for students who couldn’t pass one or two subjects in the regular board exams. They give students another chance to clear those subjects.

The CBSE will announce the dates for the 2025 Class 10 and Class 12 compartment exams after the results are announced. 

The CBSE Class 10 & Class 12 results were declared on May 13, 2025. You can now check them on the official CBSE website. cbseresults.nic.in

The CBSE Class 10 exams 2025 will be held in pen-and-paper format from February 15 to March 18, and the Class 12 exams will be held from February 15 to April 2.

What is a Compartment Exam?

A compartment exam, also called a supplementary exam or a say exam, is a second chance for students who have failed in one or two subjects but have passed the rest.

The CBSE will announce the dates for these exams after the Class 10 and Class 12 board results are out. They are usually held in July or August. Students pay a fee to take the exam conducted in the same year as the board exams.

How Many Times Can a Student Take the CBSE Compartment Exam?

A student can take the compartment exam up to three times as per CBSE rules.

  • First attempt: Right after the main exam results.
  • Second attempt: In the next year's March/April board exams.
  • Third attempt: In the same year's July/August exam window.

If students do not pass within these three attempts, they must reappear for all subjects in the next board exam.

The compartment exam is only for the subjects a student failed; they don’t have to retake the ones they passed.

When is the compartment exam held?

  • The compartment exam occurs after the Class 10 and Class 12 results are announced.
  • The application window opens about 4 days after the results and usually starts in June.
  • The application window is short, lasting only 2 to 5 days.
  • CBSE will announce the exact dates on its official website.
  • Schools must submit the list of candidates (LOC) appearing for the compartment exam online at parikshasangam.cbse.gov.in.

CBSE Compartment Exam 2025: Latest Update

The CBSE Class 10 and 12 Compartment Exams for 2025 are expected to take place from July 1 to July 15, 2025 (Tentative). This gives students a focused window to prepare and improve their scores.

  • Application Window: 

    The application process for the compartment exams will begin shortly after the CBSE main results are announced in May/June 2025. Students should keep an eye on the official CBSE website for timely updates.

  • Admit Cards: 

    Admit cards are expected to be released on June 25, 2025, and can be downloaded from the official CBSE portal.

  • Date Sheet:

    A subject-wise detailed timetable will be published by CBSE after the announcement of the main board results.

  • Results: 

    The compartment exam results are expected to be declared in August 2025.

Students who did not clear one or more subjects in the main board exams can use this opportunity to secure their academic progress without losing an academic year.

Who is Eligible for the CBSE Compartment Exam?

Students who score less than 33% in a subject in the board exams must take the compartment exam to pass. This exam is only for those who have failed in one or two subjects but have passed the rest. Students who fail in more than two subjects must reappear for all subjects in the next board exam. CBSE will announce the Class 10 and Class 12 results in May 2025.

How to Apply for the Compartment Exam?

1. For Regular Students:

  • Contact your school to apply.
  • The school will register students for the exam.

2. For Private Candidates:

  • Apply online through CBSE's official website under the "Private Candidates" section.
  • Fill in your previous exam details, personal information, and the subjects you are retaking.
  • Review and submit the form carefully.

Is the Compartment Exam Tough for Class 10 and Class 12?

The compartment exam's difficulty depends on the student's understanding of the subject and preparation. Since it focuses only on the failed subject, some students find it easier, while others feel pressured because it is their last chance to pass without repeating the year.

H3- Key Points About the Compartment Exam:

  • Focused Syllabus: You only need to study the subject you failed, which is less than the entire board exam syllabus. However, since it's just one subject, you must fully grasp the concepts.
  • High Pressure: Even though it's only one or two subjects, failing again can mean repeating the entire year, which adds to the stress.

Tips to Prepare for the Compartment Exam!

  • Review Syllabus: 

    Focus on the subjects you need to clear. Ensure you are thorough with the syllabus and understand the key concepts.

  • Practice Previous Papers: 

    Solving past years' compartment exam papers can help you understand the exam pattern and the types of questions asked.

  • Time Management: 

    Allocate specific time slots for each subject and adhere to a study schedule to cover all topics systematically.

  • Seek Help: 

    If you need help with specific topics, don't hesitate to consult teachers, tutors, or online resources.

Appearing for the compartment exams is a second chance to succeed. With diligent preparation and a positive mindset, students can successfully overcome this hurdle and continue their academic journey.
