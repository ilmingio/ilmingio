Kerala School Educational Calendar 2025–26: Check Exam Dates & Holidays

Planning your child’s academic year just got easier. Th  Kerala Education Department has officially released the Kerala school academic calendar 2025–26, covering everything from school holidays, exam dates, to major school events across the state. Whether your child is in primary, high school, or higher secondary, this calendar is your go-to guide for the year ahead.

Let’s break it down for you in simple words.

Academic Year Overview

The new school year in Kerala will begin in June 2025. Though the exact reopening date may vary slightly depending on the school, the calendar applies to all government, aided, and most private schools under the General Education Department.

From June 2025 to May 2026, schools will follow the structure given in the Kerala educational calendar 2025–26, which outlines important school events, working days, holidays, and exam schedules.

Kerala School Holidays 2025

The major school breaks and festival holidays listed in the Kerala school holidays 2025 calendar:

Vacation Periods:

  • Onam Holidays: August 29 – September 7, 2025
    Reopens on: September 8, 2025
  • Christmas Holidays: December 19 – 28, 2025
    Reopens on: December 29, 2025
  • Summer Vacation Begins: March 31, 2026

School Event Highlights

Kerala schools are not just about academics – they also host vibrant annual events that encourage student participation and creativity.

Noteworthy Events:

  • School Youth Festival (Kalolsavam):
    • Starts in August 2025
    • Sub-district: September 2025
    • District: October 2025
    • State Level: January 3–7, 2026
  • School Science Fair (Sasthrolsavam):
    • Starts in August 2025
    • Sub-district: September 2025
    • District: October 2025
    • State Level: November 7–10, 2025
  • State School Sports Festival: October 22–27, 2025
  • Special School Kalolsavam: November 27–30, 2025

Academic Days Requirement

The Kerala school calendar ensures adequate learning days for each level:

  • Lower Primary: 198 academic days
  • Upper Primary: 200 academic days
  • High Schools: 204 academic days

To meet this requirement, additional working days have been scheduled 

Ex: July 26, Aug 16, Oct 4, Oct 25, Jan 3, Jan 31 for different levels.

Thoughts for Parents and Students

The Kerala school calendar 2025–26 is thoughtfully planned to balance academic pressure with ample vacation and event time. However, always confirm with your school for any local changes, especially if you're in a private or central board school.
