No Public Exams for TN Class 11 from 2026

Starting from the 2025–26 academic year, the Tamil Nadu government has canceled the public board examinations for Class 11 (Plus One) under the new State Education Policy (SEP).

Instead of state-wide board exams, Class 11 will now act as a preparatory year for Class 12, with a focus on skill development and conceptual understanding. Students will be assessed through school-level internal assessments and competency-based tests, not a state-level board exam.

This reform aims to reduce exam-related stress and make learning more meaningful while continuing to hold public exams only for Class 10 and Class 12 students.

Download TN Half-Yearly Exam Timetable 2025-26 PDF

The official PDF for the TN half-yearly exam timetable 2025-26 has not yet been released by the Directorate of Government Examinations.

Once available, students can download it directly from the DGE website by following these steps:

  1. Visit the official website: dge.tn.gov.in
  2. Go to the “Time Table” or “Latest Notifications” section.
  3. Find the link titled “Half-Yearly Examination Timetable 2025–26.”
  4. Click to download the PDF and save it for reference.

The timetable currently available on the DGE site is for the March/April 2026 public exams, not for the December 2025 half-yearly tests.

Keep checking the website and your school’s notice board for official updates.

Preparation Tips for TN Half Yearly Exam 2025-26

Preparing for the Tamil Nadu half-yearly exam 2025 requires smart planning and consistent practice. Here are a few useful tips for students:

  • Start Revising Early: Don’t wait until the last minute. Begin revising at least a month before the exams.
  • Focus on Important Chapters: Review the chapters frequently asked in past papers.
  • Practice Previous Year Papers: It helps in understanding question patterns and time management.
  • Clarify Doubts Early: Reach out to teachers or tutors when you get stuck on a topic.
  • Stay Calm and Positive: Consistent practice and balanced rest are key to better performance.
