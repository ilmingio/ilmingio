Marking scheme of CBSE class 10 Maths

The CBSE Class 10 Board Exams for 2024-25 will be held from February 15 to March 18, as announced by the Central Board of Secondary Education. Before the exams, CBSE shares the Class 10 Paper Pattern, which explains the question format and marking scheme. This helps students understand what to expect in the exam and prepare better.

Overview of Class 10 Math marking scheme

 

The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) has announced the  marking scheme for the Class 10 board exams in 2025. It explains how marks are distributed and which topics carry more weight in each subject. This helps students focus on important areas and prepare more effectively.

CBSE has also shared the updated exam pattern, chapter-wise marking scheme, and the 2025 Math Board exam question format. Students can find the latest syllabus on the official CBSE website. 

By following this syllabus, they can plan their studies well and ensure they cover all topics properly. Here’s a simple guide to help you understand the marking scheme and prepare more effectively.

Chapters of Maths

Marks Distribution and Important Topics

The marking scheme for each subject highlights the important topics and how many marks they carry, helping students focus on what matters most. 

For example, subjects like algebra, trigonometry, and geometry have higher weightage, so it’s important to prepare them well.

 

Internal Assessments and Practical Exams

  • Along with the written exam, CBSE also conducts internal assessments for each subject.
  • These assessments include periodic tests, notebook submissions, and activities like lab work or project presentations.
  • Internal assessments carry 20 marks, while the main exam is marked out of 80.

  • These marks are added to the final grade, so performing well in internal assessments is important.

     

 

The CBSE Class 10 Math theory exam will be out of 80 marks and will last 3 hours. Students will also get 15 extra minutes to read the question paper before they begin writing.

CBSE Class 10 Maths Structure and Marking Scheme

Many students find math difficult and worry about not scoring well. But the key to improving in math is practice; the more problems you solve, the better you’ll understand the subject.

If you want to check the official details, you can visit CBSE’s website at cbse.gov.in. Look at the exam blueprint, note the important topics, and keep practicing to develop your confidence.

Tips to Prepare for CBSE Class 10 Math

  • Practice Every Day: 

    Math gets easier with regular practice. Solve past question papers and sample papers to understand the exam pattern and types of questions.

  • Focus on Important Topics: 

    Topics like Algebra, Geometry, and Trigonometry carry more marks. Make these your priority while studying to score well.

  • Manage Your Time: 

    Balance your study time between learning concepts and solving problems. Spend extra time on difficult topics and revise regularly.

  • Clear Your Doubts: 

    If you find any topic difficult, ask your teacher, discuss with friends, or use online resources to understand it better.

  • Don’t Ignore Internal Assessments: 

    These carry 20 marks and can help your final score. Take tests, assignments, and practicals seriously.
