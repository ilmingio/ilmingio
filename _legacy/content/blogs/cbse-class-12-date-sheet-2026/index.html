Step-by-Step Guide: Download CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet PDF 2025–26

Downloading the CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet is simple. Follow these steps:

  1. Visit the official CBSE website: cbse.gov.in or cbse.nic.in.
  2. Navigate to the “Examinations” or “Date Sheet” section.
  3. Click on the link for “CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2025–26”.
  4. Download the PDF file.
  5. Save it on your device for reference during your exam preparation.

Keep checking the official site for updates, as this is the tentative schedule and changes may occur.

How to Prepare for CBSE Class 12 Board Exams 2025–26: Top Tips

1. Plan Your Study Schedule

  • Use the date sheet to create a timetable.
  • Start with difficult subjects early.

2. Focus on NCERT Books

  • NCERT textbooks are the most reliable for CBSE exams.
  • Revise diagrams, definitions, and formulas.

3. Practice Past Papers

  • Solve previous years’ question papers under exam conditions.
  • Time yourself to improve speed and accuracy.

4. Take Regular Breaks

  • Study in ilmings (50–60 minutes study + 10 minutes break).
  • Avoid burnout by maintaining balance.

5. Stay Healthy

  • Eat nutritious food and stay hydrated.
  • Ensure enough sleep for optimal concentration
