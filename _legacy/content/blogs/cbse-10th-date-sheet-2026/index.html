CBSE Class 10 Admit Card 2026 – Very Important

The CBSE Class 10 admit card is expected to be released in January 2026.

Schools will download it from the CBSE portal, and students must collect it from their school. Without the admit card, you cannot appear for the board exam.

CBSE Class 10 Exam: Important Exam Day Tips

To have a stress-free exam day, students should follow these simple rules based on the CBSE Class 10 Date Sheet 2026.

  • Reach Early: Arrive at the exam centre well before the reporting time mentioned in your timetable.
  • Carry Admit Card & ID: Bring your admit card and a valid ID proof. Without these, entry is not allowed.
  • Answer Sheet Time: You will receive your answer booklet between 10:00 AM and 10:15 AM. Fill in all details carefully.
  • Reading Time: Question papers are given at 10:15 AM. Use the next 15 minutes only to read and understand the questions.
  • Writing Starts at 10:30 AM: You can begin writing your answers only after the bell at 10:30 AM.
  • Do Not Carry Gadgets: Mobile phones, calculators, smartwatches, or any electronic items are strictly not allowed inside the exam hall.

How to Download CBSE 10 Final Date Sheet 2026

Students and parents can download the timetable easily from the CBSE website.

Step 1: Visit the official CBSE website – cbse.gov.in
Step 2: Click on the Pariksha Sangam option on the homepage.
Step 3: Choose the School or Ganga section.
Step 4: Go to Exam Reference Material → Circulars 2026.
Step 5: Click on the link that says “Date Sheet for Class X and XII for Board Examinations – 2026.”
Step 6: Download the PDF file to your device.
Step 7: Take a printout of the Class 10 date sheet for easy reference.

Preparation Tips for CBSE Class 10 Students

Here are some practical tips to prepare well for the exams:

  • Make a Study Timetable – Plan subjects according to exam dates.
  • Revise NCERT Books Thoroughly – Most questions are based on NCERT.
  • Practice Sample Papers – Solve CBSE sample papers for better time management.
  • Focus on Weak Areas – Spend extra time on topics you find difficult.
  • Stay Healthy – Balanced diet and proper sleep help in concentration.
