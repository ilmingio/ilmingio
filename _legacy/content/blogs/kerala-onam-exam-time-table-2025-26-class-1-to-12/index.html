Kerala 1st Onam Exam Date Sheet 2025–26 Out! LP, UP, High School, and Plus Two

As the new academic year progresses, schools across Kerala are gearing up for the First Term (Onam) Examination 2025. These exams are an important milestone for students from Class 1 to 12, helping schools assess academic progress before the festive Onam holidays. The Kerala First Term Onam exams will be held from August 18 to August 28, 2025.

The Kerala Education Department has officially released the Kerala Onam exam timetable 2025, and it applies to students across LP (Lower Primary), UP (Upper Primary), High School (Classes 8–10), and Higher Secondary (Plus Two). 

For parents, students, or educators looking for accurate information on Kerala's first-term exam date in 2025, this article has everything you need, from the full exam schedule to Onam celebration dates, passing criteria, and post-exam holidays.

Kerala First Term Exam 2025 – Overview for Classes 1 to 12

The First Term Exams (Onam Exams) will be held in all schools following the Kerala State Syllabus, covering classes from 1 to 12. These exams will take place over a week and will be conducted in two sessions, forenoon (morning) and afternoon.

The exam schedule is applicable for:

  • LP & UP students (Classes 1 to 7)
  • High School students (Classes 8 to 10)
  • Plus Two students (Class 12 – DHSE Kerala)

Quick summary of important dates:

New Passing Marks Rule: 30% Required in Each Subject

Kerala has introduced new passing rules for the Onam exams. From now on, students in Classes 8, 10 (SSLC), and 12 (Plus Two) must score at least 30% in every subject to pass. 

The earlier “all-pass” system has been removed, which means each subject is important for promotion. Students who fall short of 30% will not be failed immediately. Instead, schools will arrange special support classes to help them improve, followed by a supplementary SAY exam. 

This new rule ensures every child has strong subject knowledge before moving to the next class.

Kerala LP & UP Onam Exam 2025 Timetable for Classes 1 to 7

For Lower Primary (Classes 1–4) and Upper Primary (Classes 5–7) students, the exam timetable is officially announced by the Kerala Education Department. These students will appear for various language and subject papers over a few days, with exams scheduled in morning and afternoon sessions.

Class 1 and 2 students will have flexible exam timings based on activity completion.

Kerala High School Onam Exam Timetable 2025 for Classes 8, 9, 10

Students in Classes 8, 9, and 10 (SSLC) will follow the Kerala High School Onam exam 2025 timetable issued by the Education Department. The exams include core subjects such as English, Social Science, Mathematics, and languages. Special care has been taken to ensure a well-paced schedule, with adequate time between major papers.

Cool-off time is included in exam duration. Afternoon exams on Fridays begin at 2:00 PM.

Kerala Plus Two Onam Exam Timetable 2025 for Class 12 DHSE

The Kerala Plus Two Onam exam dates for 2025 have been released for students under the DHSE board. Exams will be held from August 18 to August 27, 2025, primarily in the afternoon session, except for a few subjects like English and Physics.

Onam Celebrations Begin Across Kerala from August 29

After the exam season, schools across Kerala begin their Onam celebrations on August 29, 2025. The festive spirit is alive with cultural programs, traditional games, flower arrangements (Pookkalam), and special assemblies. These events are an essential part of student life, promoting unity and joy in schools across the state.

Onam Holidays 2025-26 in Kerala

After the exams, students can enjoy the much-awaited Onam holidays from August 29 to September 7, 2025. This break allows students to participate in family functions, cultural events, and celebrations. Schools will reopen on September 8, 2025, marking the beginning of the second term.
