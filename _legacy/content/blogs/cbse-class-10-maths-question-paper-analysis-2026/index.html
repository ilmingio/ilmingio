CBSE Math Exam Review: CBSE Class 10 Maths Paper Analysis 2026

The CBSE Class 10 Maths board exam 2026 was successfully conducted today, February 17, 2026, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM across India. Students were given an extra 15 minutes from 10:15 AM to read the question paper before starting the exam.

Now that the exam is over, students and teachers are discussing the difficulty level, important questions, and overall paper pattern. This CBSE Class 10 Maths paper analysis 2026 gives a complete review of the exam, including section-wise analysis, key topics covered, and CBSE Class 10 Maths exam today student reactions.

 

 

Overall, the paper was balanced, NCERT-based, and scoring for students who had prepared well and practiced sample papers.

CBSE Class 10 Maths Exam 2026: Important Details

Here are the key details of the CBSE Class 10 Maths board exam 2026:

The paper followed the official CBSE exam pattern and was based mainly on the NCERT syllabus.

CBSE Class 10 Maths Paper Analysis 2026: Overall Difficulty Levelb

According to most students and teachers, the overall difficulty level of the exam was easy to moderate.

Here is the complete Class 10 Maths exam review 17 February 2026 based on student feedback:

  • MCQs were easy and scoring
  • Most questions were directly from NCERT concepts
  • Some algebra and geometry questions were tricky
  • Long answer questions were lengthy
  • The paper required good time management

Students who practiced NCERT examples, exercises, and CBSE sample papers found the exam manageable.

The paper tested both concept clarity and application skills, which is part of CBSE’s competency-based exam pattern.

Section-Wise Analysis of CBSE Class 10 Maths Question Paper 2026

The question paper was divided into five sections: A, B, C, D, and E.

Section A: MCQs (1 Mark Each)

  • Total Questions: 20
  • Difficulty Level: Easy
  • Most questions were direct and formula-based
  • Assertion-Reason questions required careful reading

This was the most scoring section for students.

Section B: Very Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)

  • Total Questions: 5
  • Difficulty Level: Easy to Moderate
  • Questions were concept-based
  • Mostly from Algebra and Trigonometry

Students who understood concepts clearly were able to solve quickly.

Section C: Short Answer Questions (3 Marks Each)

  • Total Questions: 6
  • Difficulty Level: Moderate
  • Included application-based questions
  • Required step-by-step solutions

Some students found this section slightly time-consuming.

Section D: Long Answer Questions (5 Marks Each)

  • Total Questions: 4
  • Difficulty Level: Moderate to Difficult
  • Lengthy calculations required
  • One geometry question was slightly tricky

This section required strong understanding and proper time management.

Section E: Case Study Questions (4 Marks Each)

  • Total Questions: 3
  • Difficulty Level: Easy to Moderate
  • Based on real-life situations
  • Mostly from Statistics and Geometry

Students who practiced sample papers found this section familiar.

Student reactions play an important role in CBSE Class 10 Maths paper analysis 2026.

Overall Reaction: Easy to Moderate

Many students said the paper was easier than expected and mostly based on NCERT.

Familiar Questions

Lengthy Paper

The most common feedback was that the paper was lengthy, especially Section D.

Some students said they had to manage time carefully to complete all questions.

MCQs Were Scoring

Students found MCQs simple and helpful in scoring good marks.

Standard vs Basic Maths Reaction

  • Standard Maths: Moderate difficulty, some tricky questions
  • Basic Maths: Easier compared to Standard Maths

Overall, students who prepared well felt confident after the exam.

Passing Marks and Grading Criteria

To pass the CBSE Class 10 Maths exam, students must score:

  • Minimum 33% overall
  • Must pass both theory and internal assessment

CBSE may give grace marks in certain situations.

Students who fail in one or two subjects can appear for compartment exams.
