  • Oct. 30, 2025, 5:35 p.m.

Kerala DHSE confirms Plus One exam schedule for March 2026

The wait is finally over! Kerala’s Education Minister, V. Sivankutty, has officially revealed the 2026 board exam schedule for Plus One (Class 11) students.

As per the announcement, the Kerala Plus One public examinations 2026 will be held from March 5 to March 27, 2026.

The results are likely to be declared by May 2026, allowing students sufficient time to gear up for their Plus Two studies. The comprehensive official timetable will soon be published on the Directorate of Higher Secondary Education (DHSE) Kerala website and the Pareeksha Bhavan portal.

This announcement offers much-needed clarity for both students and parents, enabling them to organize their preparation strategically during this important phase of their academic journey.

Plus One Public Exam 2026 Overview 

  • Plus One refers to Class 11 (first year of higher secondary education) covering streams such as Science, Commerce, Arts under Kerala DHSE.
  • The public exam is held at the end of this year (for example, in March) and determines progress towards Class 12.
  • For many students, this is the first major board-level public exam, so having clarity on dates and format is key.

How to Download the Timetable & PDF

Here is the simple sequence for students and parents:

  1. Visit the DHSE Kerala website dhsekerala.gov.in.
  2. Look for the link “Plus One Time Table 2026” or “HSE 1st Year Time Table 2026”.
  3. Click to open a PDF which lists all subjects, dates, timings, instructions.
  4. Download and keep a printed copy for home reference.
  5. Check with your school if they provide a printed schedule or notice board update.

What Students & Parents Should Prepare For Kerala Plusone Exam 2026

  • Students should complete syllabus units early, especially for those subjects holding early exam dates.
  • Download the timetable and mark each exam date; include travel time if the student goes to another centre.
  • Parents: check transport, meal and rest arrangements for your child during this period.
  • Schools typically offer model exams before the public exam – use those to track progress and identify weak areas.
  • Ensure students carry hall ticket/admit card and follow exam-day protocols (no gadgets, be punctual, etc).
  • Encourage regular breaks, sleep, and avoid last-minute panics.

Smart Study Plan Suggestions for Students Preparing for Kerala Plus one Exam 2026

  • Use the timetable to create a daily revision schedule: e.g., from today until first exam: 50% new topics, 50% revision.
  • After each exam: spend a short time reviewing how you did, then rest and begin preparing for the next.
  • Use group study or peer assessment for tricky subjects; parents can help the student structure such sessions.
  • Use past papers and model exams to familiarise with question types and timing.
  • Stay physically and mentally fit: good sleep, healthy food, and a positive mindset.
