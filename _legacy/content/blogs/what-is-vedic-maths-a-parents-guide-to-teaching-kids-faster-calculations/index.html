What is Vedic Maths?

Vedic Maths is an old method of solving math problems that comes from the ancient Indian scriptures called the Vedas. It was simplified and shared with the world in the 20th century by Swami Bharati Krishna Tirthaji.

This method is built on 16 main formulas (sutras) and 13 smaller formulas (sub-sutras). These are simple rules that help solve everything from basic addition to algebra and geometry.

But here’s the best part—Vedic Maths makes calculations much faster and easier, especially when done in the head. That means less time with pen and paper and more time understanding and enjoying math!

History of Vedic Maths

Vedic Maths is an ancient method of doing maths in a faster and easier way.
It is believed to be based on the old Indian texts called the Vedas, especially the Atharva Veda.

  • The word Vedic comes from Veda, which means “knowledge” or “wisdom” in Sanskrit.
  • The Vedas are some of the oldest books in the world.
  • They include knowledge about science, maths, philosophy, and more.

Ancient Roots

  • Vedic Maths is thought to have been used in India over 5,000 years ago.
  • Back then, knowledge was not written down but shared through speaking and memory.
  • Maths was part of the Vedangas; subjects that helped people understand the Vedas.

Rediscovery in Modern Times

Vedic Maths as we know it today was put together by Swami Bharati Krishna Tirthaji Maharaj in the early 1900s.

  • He was a scholar and spiritual leader.
  • He studied old Indian texts for many years.
  • Through deep thinking and research, he came up with:
    • 16 main formulas (called Sutras)
    • 13 smaller formulas (called Sub-sutras)
  • He believed these could solve all kinds of maths problems.
  • His book, Vedic Mathematics, was published in 1965 after his death.

Why Vedic Maths is Special

  • It focuses on solving problems quickly and mentally.
  • It uses simple tricks to handle even tough problems.
  • It’s very different from the long and slow methods we usually learn in school.

Why Should Kids Learn Vedic Maths?

Here’s why Vedic Maths is a great skill for your child:

  • Super-fast Calculations: It can make solving math problems up to 10-15 times faster.
  • Builds Confidence: Quick and correct answers give kids confidence and reduce math anxiety.
  • Improves Focus: It enhances memory, concentration, and logical thinking.
  • Useful Across Topics: Whether it's addition or algebra, these tricks apply everywhere.
  • Fun Learning: Kids love the "magic" of Vedic Maths—it feels more like a game than a lesson!

How Can Parents Teach Vedic Maths at Home?

You don’t have to be a math expert! Here’s how you can help your child:

  1. Start Simple: Begin with easy tricks like squaring or multiplying by 11.
  2. Make It Fun: Use games, puzzles, and flashcards.
  3. Encourage Mental Math: Ask your child to solve problems in their head.
    Use Learning Tools: Try online videos, apps, and printed worksheets.
  4. Practice Daily: Even 10 minutes a day can make a big difference.
  5. Apply in Daily Life: Use Vedic Maths while shopping or cooking to show real-world use.

The Father of the Nation’s Mental Math Movement

When we talk about Vedic Maths today, one name always stands out;  Swami Bharati Krishna Tirthaji Maharaj. He is known as the Father of Vedic Maths because he brought this powerful method back to life in the early 1900s.

  • Born: 1884
  • Died: 1960
  • Position: 143rd Shankaracharya of Govardhan Math, Puri
  • Vedic Maths was invented by Swami Bharati Krishna Tirthaji Maharaj in the early 1900s.
