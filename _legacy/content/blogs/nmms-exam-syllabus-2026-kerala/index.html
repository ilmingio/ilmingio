A Complete Way to NMMS Success in 2026

Every year, thousands of students across India, especially in Kerala aim to qualify for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) Exam 2026 that rewards academic excellence and financial need.

We’ll explore everything you need to know about the NMMS syllabus 2026 Kerala, how to access and download the NMMS exam syllabus PDF, and what the NMMS exam subjects actually include. Whether you’re looking for the exact topics to study, preparation tips, or state-specific details this article will guide you step by step.

NMMS Syllabus 2026 – Overview

The NMMS exam typically has two main papers: the Mental Ability Test (MAT) and the Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT).

Each state, including Kerala implements its version of the syllabus in line with these two papers.

  • Paper 1: MAT – tests reasoning, patterns, and logic.
  • Paper 2: SAT – tests scholastic subjects: Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies (or Social Science) for classes ~7/8.
  • While there’s no fixed statewide syllabus document for every topic, students are advised to cover the Class 7 & Class 8 school syllabus well.

In Kerala, the state body State Council of Educational Research and Training, Kerala (SCERT) handles the NMMS exam details. 

Hence, for the 2026 exam, students should study their school syllabus up to Class 8 thoroughly and practice reasoning for MAT.

NMMS Syllabus 2026 Kerala – Class-Wise Guide

Here’s how to structure your preparation by class. Though the actual exam is for students around Class 8 level, covering Class 7 content strengthens your base.

For Class 7 

Even if the exam is for Class 8, many topics from Class 7 are included. Covering them ensures you are well-prepared. Important topics to cover:

  • Science: Nutrition in Plants, Nutrition in Animals, Fibre to Fabric, Heat, Winds/Storms etc.
  • Mathematics: Basics of numbers, algebraic expressions, data handling, geometry introduction.
  • Social Studies: Basic civics, geography, environment, resources.
  • MAT: Start practising reasoning, patterns, analogies.

NMMS Syllabus 2026 – Subject-Wise Deep Dive

Let’s now go deeper into each subject area, with point-wise lists you can tick off.

Mathematics

  • Rational numbers, operations and properties
  • Squares, square roots, cubes, cube roots
  • Data handling: collection, representation (graphs/charts)
  • Algebraic expressions and identities
  • Exponents & powers
  • Linear equations in one variable
  • Mensuration: area & volume of practical shapes
  • Practical geometry: constructions, quadrilaterals
  • Visualising solid shapes: cubes, cuboids, cylinders
  • Introduction to graphs and comparing quantities

Science

  • Materials: Metals, non-metals, their properties, and uses
  • Combustion and flame, chemical changes
  • Chemical effects of electric current
  • Cell—Structure and functions, reproduction in animals/plants
  • Force and pressure, sound, stars and solar system
  • Conservation of plants and animals, environment
  • Weather, climate, adaptation of animals
  • Physical and chemical changes
  • Water as a resource, pollution of air and water

Social Studies / Social Science

  • Indian Constitution & secularism
  • Parliament, making of laws, judiciary
  • Social justice, marginalised communities
  • Economy: markets, government’s role
  • Geography: land, soil, water resources, natural vegetation
  • History: colonialism, changes in art & culture
  • Visual arts, women, caste, reform movements

Mental Ability Test (MAT)

  • Analogy (verbal & non-verbal)
  • Classification (grouping, sorting)
  • Numerical series (find next number)
  • Pattern perception (visual patterns)
  • Hidden figures (identify shapes hidden)
  • Verbal reasoning: maybe English/Hindi proficiency (in some states)

Study Plan & Preparation Tips for NMMS 2026

Here are actionable tips for parents and students at ilming.io to follow:

  • Begin early: Start with Class 7 topics to build strength, then move on to Class 8.
  • Create a weekly schedule: Dedicate days to MAT practice (reasoning) and days to SAT subjects.
  • Use the syllabus tables above to tick off topics as they are completed.
  • Download the NMMS syllabus PDF for Kerala and refer regularly.
  • Practice previous year question papers (especially for Kerala) to become familiar with format.
  • Focus on weaker areas early (e.g., if Math is weak, give extra time).
  • Encourage regular breaks, healthy sleep, and consistency—not last minute rush.
  • Use simple language materials (that’s why this blog is in user-friendly style) so students don’t feel overwhelmed.
  • Parents should monitor progress, help remove distractions, and keep morale high.
