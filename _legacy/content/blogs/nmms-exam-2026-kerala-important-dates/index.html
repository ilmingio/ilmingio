Your Complete Guide to NMMS Exam 2026 Kerala

The NMMS exam is a wonderful opportunity for bright students in Class 8 in Kerala who meet the eligibility criteria. With the right guidance and timely preparation, the exam becomes manageable rather than overwhelming. Parents can support their child by knowing the key dates, timetable, and details

In this blog let’s discuss the details of the NMMS exam date for 2026 in Kerala, the timetable you should follow, and the full exam schedule, from eligibility to timeline to how our crash course at Ilming Learning can help you succeed.

Overview of NMMS Scholarship Exam 2026

  • The NMMS scheme is designed to provide a scholarship to meritorious students from economically weaker backgrounds so they can continue their education from secondary to higher secondary level. 
  • In Kerala, the exam is conducted by the State Council of Educational Research and Training Kerala (SCERT Kerala)
  • Kerala NMMS Exam 2026 is scheduled for December 30, 2025, and serves as a key scholarship opportunity for deserving Class 8 students. 
  • The exam consists of two parts: the Mental Ability Test (MAT) and the Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT). These are typically multiple choice and time-bound.
  • Scholarship amount, eligibility criteria and continuation conditions apply. For example, students who qualify must maintain certain percentages in subsequent classes.

NMMS Timetable 2026 – What to Expect for Kerala

Kerala NMMS Timetable 2026 features a straightforward schedule with two main sessions on December 30, 2025. Students should expect a well-organized exam day, divided into SAT and MAT papers conducted by SCERT Kerala.

How to use this timetable for preparation:

  • Plan for a short break between the two sessions; you’ll want to rest, hydrate, and mentally prepare for the second paper.
  • Simulate the exam day in mock tests: two papers back-to-back with a lunch break.
  • Focus on time management: both papers are generally of 90 minutes each with ~90 questions each (depending on state).

Kerala NMMS Exam Schedule – Step-by-Step Timeline for Students & Parents

Here’s a user-friendly schedule you and your child can follow, beginning several months ahead. This makes it easy to track what needs to be done when.

6-8 months before exam 

  • The student confirms their Class 8 enrollment in a recognized school in Kerala.
  • Parent verifies eligibility: income certificate (≤ ₹3.5 lakh/annum), marks in Class 7 (≈55% for general, 50% for SC/ST) etc.\
  • Set up a study plan: include NMMS-specific topics, and join a crash course like at Ilming Learning.

3-4 months before exam

  • Application forms are released, and the student applies through school before the last date.
  • Collect necessary documents: parent income proof, passport-size photo, and school record.
  • Register for the NMMS crash course at Ilming Learning to begin focused preparation.

1–2 months before exam 

  • The admit card is issued. Students checks details carefully: name, roll number, exam centre.
  • The timetable is reviewed; plan travel to the exam centre if needed.
  • Intensive revision begins: mock tests, previous year papers, time management.
  • Ilming Learning’s NMMS crash course runs full speed: daily tests, doubt-clearing, tips for MAT & SAT.

Exam day:

  • First session: SAT (morning).
  • Break.
  • Second session: MAT (afternoon).
  • Student carries admit card, necessary stationery and arrives early at exam centre.
  • Parents provide support: ensure transport, calm environment.

Post-exam (Jan–March):

  • The provisional answer key is likely released; students can compare answers.
  • The final answer key & results are announced in March; selected students get the scholarship.
  • Students who qualifies moves on with motivation; Ilming Learning can support continued classes for higher grades.

Tips for Students & Parents to Stay on Track

  • Mark the application and exam dates in a wall calendar or digital reminder.
  • Make sure the student’s Class 7 marks and eligibility are confirmed well in advance.
  • Encourage the child to create a daily timetable, e.g., one hour for MAT reasoning and one for SAT math/science/social.
  • Practice under timed conditions: simulate 90-minute paper sessions.
  • For MAT: focus on non-verbal reasoning, series, analogy, and hidden figures.
  • For the SAT: revise Class 7-8 math, science, and social science thoroughly.
  • On exam day: ensure proper sleep the night before, have breakfast, and reach centre early.
  • Parents: avoid pressure; encourage steady progress and a calm mindset.

In Kerala, the NMMS exam is conducted by the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT Kerala).

The NMMS Kerala 2026 exam is expected to be conducted on December 30, 2026. Students should start preparing well in advance.

Students studying in Class 8 in a government, government-aided, or local body school can apply. They must have, At least 55% marks in Class 7 (50% for SC/ST/PH students), Parental annual income not exceeding ₹3.5 lakh

The exam has two papers conducted on the same day, MAT (Mental Ability Test) – Tests reasoning and thinking ability, SAT (Scholastic Aptitude Test) – Tests subjects like Science, Maths, and Social Science. Both papers have multiple-choice questions.
