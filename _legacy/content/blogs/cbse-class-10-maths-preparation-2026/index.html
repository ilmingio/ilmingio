Month-by-Month Class 10 Maths Study Plan (5 Months)

How to Score 95 in Maths Class 10 CBSE – Step-by-Step

1. Stick to NCERT First
Every question in the board exam is based on NCERT concepts. Don’t skip any example or exercise.

2. Practice Daily for 1–1.5 Hours
Short, consistent sessions work better than marathon study hours.

3. Use the CBSE Class 10 Maths Sample Paper 2026
Solving sample papers improves speed and accuracy while familiarizing you with real exam patterns.

4. Maintain a Formula Notebook
List all important formulas, theorems, and identities in one place for daily revision.

5. Focus on Important Topics
Key chapters with higher weightage include:

  • Trigonometry
  • Quadratic Equations
  • Surface Areas and Volumes
  • Statistics & Probability
  • Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

6. Solve Previous Year Papers
Practicing 10 CBSE Maths Question Paper sets builds exam confidence.

7. Revise Smartly
Instead of re-reading everything, revise key questions, mistakes, and formulas.

8. Stay Calm and Take Breaks
A stress-free mind performs better. Sleep well and take short breaks to refresh.

Class 10 Maths Study Plan 5 Months – Weekly Routine Example

Common Mistakes Students Should Avoid

  • Skipping basic NCERT examples
  • Not reviewing incorrect answers
  • Focusing only on tough chapters and ignoring easy scoring ones
  • Leaving diagrams, steps, or units incomplete
  • Over-stressing before exams

Smart Time Management Tips for the Exam

  • Read the entire paper in the first 10 minutes
  • Attempt easier questions first
  • Allocate roughly 40 minutes for each 20-mark section
  • Leave the last 15 minutes for revision
  • Show all steps clearly, marks are awarded for steps in Maths

Last-Minute Preparation Tips Before the CBSE Exam

  • Focus only on important formulas and diagrams
  • Avoid learning new topics in the last week
  • Solve one 10 CBSE Maths Sample Paper every day
  • Stay hydrated and relaxed, your brain works best when calm
