Kerala LSS Exam 2026: How to Apply

The Lower Secondary Scholarship (LSS) Exam 2026 Kerala is one of the most-awaited scholarship exams for Class 4 students across the state. Conducted by the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan, this exam identifies academically talented students and provides them with financial assistance to continue their education confidently.

If your child is in Class 4 and studying under the Kerala State syllabus, this guide covers everything you need to know — from the expected LSS exam date 2026 Kerala, to the timetable, syllabus, and application process.

What Is the LSS Exam?

The Lower Secondary Scholarship (LSS) exam is a state-level merit scholarship test conducted by the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan. It’s designed for Class 4 students studying in government, aided, and recognized unaided schools in Kerala.

The main goal of the exam is to identify bright young learners early and provide financial support for their studies. Selected students receive a scholarship of ₹1,000 per year up to Class 7, helping them stay motivated and focused on academic growth.

LSS Exam 2026 Kerala – Important Dates (Expected)

The official LSS Exam Date 2026 Kerala has not yet been announced. However, based on the 2025 cycle (exam held on February 27, 2025), the LSS 2026 exam is expected to take place in February 2026.

Parents and schools should keep checking the Pareeksha Bhavan LSS/USS page and school circulars for the official notification and exact dates.

Kerala LSS Exam 2026 – Timetable & Format

The exam is usually conducted in a single sitting, covering four main subjects:

  • Malayalam
  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies (EVS)

It follows an objective (MCQ) format, and the questions are designed based on the SCERT Class 4 syllabus. Each question tests the student’s understanding, reasoning, and application skills rather than rote learning.

Exact bell timings, subject sequence, and reporting instructions are shared by schools once the official circular is released by Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan.

Eligibility Criteria for Kerala LSS Exam

To appear for the LSS Exam Kerala 2026, students must:

  • Be studying in Class 4 in a government, aided, or recognized unaided school in Kerala.
  • Be enrolled in a school recognized by the Department of Education.
  • Have good academic standing as per school records.

There’s no direct registration for students. All applications are submitted through schools via the official Pareeksha Bhavan LSS/USS online portal.

LSS Exam Syllabus 2026 Kerala

The LSS exam syllabus 2026 is entirely based on the SCERT Class 4 textbooks. The syllabus includes:

  • Malayalam – reading comprehension, grammar, and language skills.
  • English – vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, and sentence formation.
  • Mathematics – arithmetic operations, patterns, fractions, measurements, and problem-solving.
  • Environmental Studies (EVS) – concepts from nature, society, and environment as per Class 4 lessons.

All questions are objective/MCQ-based and test both conceptual understanding and logical reasoning.

Preparation Tips for LSS Exam 

  • Revise Class 4 SCERT textbooks thoroughly.
  • Practice previous year’s question papers and sample OMR sheets shared by schools.
  • Focus on EVS application-based questions and core arithmetic in Maths.
  • Strengthen comprehension and grammar sections in both Malayalam and English.

How to Apply for the Kerala LSS Exam 2026

Parents do not need to apply directly for the LSS Exam. The application process is entirely handled by the school authorities.

Step-by-Step Process (for Schools)

  1. Log in to the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan LSS/USS portal when the registration window opens (expected January 2026).
  2. Enter and verify student details — name, date of birth, class, school code, and category.
  3. Generate the confirmation printout and keep it for school records.

What Parents Should Do

  • Submit the required details and recent photograph (if asked) to the class teacher.
  • Verify the child’s name, DOB, and school details carefully, as they appear in the admit card and result.
  • Confirm with the school that the student’s details have been uploaded before the deadline.

Admit Card and Exam Center for LSS

Once the application process is complete, admit cards will be available online through the Pareeksha Bhavan portal.
Schools will distribute printed hall tickets containing:

  • Exam date and time
  • Reporting instructions
  • Exam center details

Students must follow the instructions carefully and reach the exam center on time.

LSS Exam Scholarship Benefits

Students who qualify for the LSS Scholarship 2026 receive ₹1,000 per year till Class 7, provided they maintain the required academic standards and complete verification at school.

In 2025, document verification for selected students was completed by mid-June, and a similar timeline can be expected in 2026.

A Quick Look at USS Exam Syllabus 2026 (For Reference)

Alongside LSS (Class 4), the Upper Secondary Scholarship (USS) exam is conducted for Class 7 students.
The USS syllabus 2026 is based on the SCERT Class 7 curriculum and includes two papers:

  • Paper I: First Language and English
  • Paper II: Mathematics, Science, and Social Science

LSS and USS help nurture academic excellence and provide deserving students with early recognition and support.

Preparation Tips for LSS Exam 2026 Kerala

  • Track the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan LSS/USS page for official updates.
  • Plan weekly revision using model question papers.
  • Practice solving OMR sheets within the time limit.
  • Clarify concepts instead of memorizing answers.
  • Build consistency; a few minutes of daily revision helps more than last-minute cramming.
