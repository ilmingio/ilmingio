TN Board Exams 2026: 10th, 11th, & 12th Time Tables Announced

The Directorate of Government Examinations, Tamil Nadu (TNDGE) has officially released the Tamil Nadu SSLC, Plus One, and Plus Two Time Tables 2026. The complete subject-wise schedule is now available on the official website — dge.tn.gov.in.

According to the official press release, the SSLC (Class 10) exams will be held from March 11 to April 6, 2026, while the Plus Two (Class 12) exams are scheduled from March 2 to 26, 2026. The Plus One (Class 11) public examinations for arrear candidates will take place from March 3 to 27, 2026.

 

 

Students can now plan their preparation in advance by referring to the detailed exam dates, timing, and subject order. The following sections cover the complete schedule, practical dates, and helpful study tips for TN Board Exams 2026.

Minimum Passing Marks for Tamil Nadu Board Exams 2026

To qualify in the Tamil Nadu board exams, students must meet the following minimum marks criteria:

SSLC :

  • Minimum 35 marks in each subject.
  • Internal + theory combined score should be at least 35 out of 100.

Plus One & Plus Two :

  • Minimum 35% in each subject (theory + practical combined).
  • Students must pass both theory and practical components separately, wherever applicable.

Failing to meet these minimum marks may require appearing for the supplementary exams conducted by TNDGE.

Where to Download TN SSLC, Plus One & Plus Two Time Table 2026 PDF

Students can easily access the official timetables by following these steps:

  1. Visit the official website: dge.tn.gov.in
  2. Click on the “Time Table” section on the homepage.
  3. Select the respective exam (SSLC / HSE +1 / HSE +2).
  4. Download and save the PDF for future reference.

How to Prepare for Tamil Nadu Board Exams 2026

Smart preparation can help students score well without stress. Here are some effective strategies:

  • Use Active Recall: Revise by testing yourself using flashcards or quiz-style notes.
  • Follow Spaced Repetition: Review important concepts after 1, 3, and 7 days for long-term retention.
  • Practice Previous Year Papers: Solve at least 5–6 years’ question papers to understand question patterns.
  • Mock Tests: Take full-length mock exams every weekend to build exam stamina.
  • Focus on Weak Areas: Identify and revise your low-performing subjects first.
  • Stay Consistent: Study a bit every day instead of long, tiring sessions once in a while.

How to Manage Time Effectively While Studying for Board Exams

Good time management is key to reducing stress and improving performance. Try these tips:

  1. Make a Study Calendar: Allot subjects to specific days and fix daily revision targets.
  2. Use the Pomodoro Technique: Study for 25 minutes and take a 5-minute break — repeat 4 times for a focused hour.
  3. Prioritize Tough Subjects: Start your day with the subjects you find most challenging.
  4. Avoid Multitasking: Focus on one subject or topic at a time for better retention.
  5. Take Regular Breaks: Rest your eyes and mind every hour; short breaks increase concentration.
  6. Track Your Progress Weekly: Review what you’ve covered and adjust your plan if needed.
