What is a Classroom Program? Personalized One-on-One Tutoring for Students in Grades 1-12

Our Academic Tuition program offers personalized one-on-one online classroom learning aligned with NCERT, SCERT, and NIOS syllabi, designed for students from Class 1 to 12. We cover all major subjects including Maths, English, Science, and more, with classes customized to each student’s learning needs.

Parents can choose support for all subjects or focus on a single subject based on academic requirements. Each student is matched with experienced tutors who provide dedicated guidance and structured learning support. Along with regular academic coaching, our online classroom program also includes online crash courses for board exams, covering CBSE, ICSE, State, IGCSE, and other syllabi.

 

 

Whether your child needs consistent academic tuition, focused exam preparation, or extra help to overcome learning challenges and develop essential skills, this Academic Tuition program is designed to support long-term academic success and confidence.

Ilming's Classroom Program That Makes Learning Enjoyable

At Ilming Learning, we offer a unique program to provide personalized, one-on-one teaching for students. Here's what makes us stand out:

1] ONE-ON-ONE PERSONALIZED TEACHING

Each student gets individual one-on-one classes designed according to their learning speed, syllabus, and academic needs. Tutors focus on the student’s strengths and work on weaker areas to improve understanding and confidence.

2] PROPER INDIVIDUAL ASSESSMENT & REPORT GENERATION

Students go through regular assessments to understand their subject knowledge and learning gaps. Based on the results, detailed reports are created to highlight strengths, improvement areas, and clear action plans for better progress.

3] INDUCTION

The induction session helps students and parents understand the learning platform, class structure, schedules, and expectations. Conducted by our CRO, it ensures a smooth start and builds comfort with tutors and the learning process.

4] PERSONALIZED MENTOR SUPPORT

Each student receives dedicated mentor support based on their goals and challenges. Mentors guide, motivate, and monitor progress to ensure continuous academic improvement.

5] FREE DEMO CLASS

Demo classes allow students and parents to experience our teaching style, tutor interaction, and online classroom environment. This helps in making a confident and informed decision before enrolment.

6] PERSONALIZED ACADEMIC CALENDAR

A customized academic plan is created based on the student’s syllabus, learning pace, and exam goals. This ensures structured learning, timely revision, and effective exam preparation.

7] INDIVIDUAL WHATSAPP GROUP

A separate WhatsApp group is created for each student, including tutors and coordinators. It is used for daily updates, guidance, feedback, and communication, ensuring transparency and continuous support.

8] CUSTOMIZED CLASSROOM STUDY RESOURCES

Study materials are customized to the student’s syllabus, learning level, and exam needs. This includes worksheets, practice questions, revision notes, and writing support for focused learning.

9] STUDENT IMPACT REPORT

The student impact report highlights real academic and skill improvements over time. It focuses on learning progress, confidence building, and behavioral changes, not just marks.

10] COUNSELLING SERVICES

Counselling support helps students manage academic stress, exam anxiety, and confidence issues. Sessions focus on motivation, goal setting, and emotional well-being for balanced learning.

11] FLEXIBLE TIMINGS FOR PARENTS

Class timings are flexible and planned according to the availability of students and parents. This ensures learning fits smoothly into school and family schedules.

12] FEEDBACK CALLS

Regular feedback calls are conducted to update parents about academic progress, attendance, and learning behavior. These discussions help parents stay informed and involved in their child’s learning journey.

13] ALL KIND OF SYLLABUSES

We support all major syllabuses including CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, IGCSE, and NIOS. Teaching methods and materials are adapted to match each syllabus and exam pattern.

14] STUDENT ENGAGING SESSIONS

Classes are interactive and engaging, encouraging students to participate actively. This improves understanding, focus, and long-term retention while making learning enjoyable.

15] EXAM-ORIENTED SESSIONS

Special sessions focus on exam patterns, time management, answer writing, and revision techniques. Students learn scoring strategies and common mistakes to perform better in exams.

16] SMED (Acadamic team) SUPPORT FOR STUDENTS DOUBT CLEARANCE

Quick and structured doubt-clearing support is provided by subject experts. This ensures students get timely help and continue learning without confusion or delay.

Our goal is to help every student excel academically while making the learning experience enjoyable and stress-free.

Our Academic Tuition classroom program provides well-organized, syllabus-aligned study materials to support consistent learning and academic success. All materials are systematically arranged to ensure clarity, continuity, and better understanding across subjects and grades.

  6. Revision & Booster Materials
    Focused revision resources and extra practice materials to support exam preparation and long-term retention.

These structured learning resources ensure students receive complete academic support as part of our personalized one-on-one online classroom learning.

Class Schedule and Timings: Flexible Online One-on-One Learning Plans

  1. Classes will be held online, live, and one-on-one with your assigned tutor.
  2. Each class will usually last 1.5 hours, or 2 hours for higher secondary students.
  3. The class schedule will be confirmed by the Client Relation Officer (CRO), and students or parents can request changes by communicating with the CRO.
  4. Students and parents should log in and join the class on time.
  5. If the student is more than 10 minutes late, the tutor may choose to skip or reschedule the class.
  6. If the student can’t attend a class, the parent must inform the CRO at least 24 hours in advance.
