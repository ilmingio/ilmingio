Online Home Schooling Program for Classes 1 to 8

Ilming Learnin's Online Home Schooling offers a structured and flexible learning model for students from Class 1 to 8. Our program follows a CBSE-mapped curriculum and is designed to give students personalized academic support through live one-on-one sessions.

This online schooling model combines structure, flexibility, and individual attention to help students build strong concepts and grow with confidence.

 

 

What is Online Home Schooling?

Online home schooling is a structured learning system where students study from home through live, guided classes instead of attending a physical school daily. It allows students to learn at their own pace while following a clear academic plan.

At Ilming, our home school model focuses on:

●  Child-paced learning

●  Strong conceptual understanding

●  Live 1:1 teaching

●  Regular academic structure

●  Continuous support

Instead of rushing to complete the syllabus, we focus on helping students understand each topic clearly before moving forward. This makes learning more meaningful and long-lasting.

Our online home schooling program is ideal for families looking for a flexible yet disciplined academic system.

How Our Teaching Model Works

The strength of our online home schooling lies in personalized learning. Every student learns through live 1:1 sessions with an experienced educator.

Live One-on-One Classes

Each student receives full attention during class. Teachers adjust their teaching style based on the student’s understanding level. This ensures clarity and comfort while learning.

Child-Paced Learning

Not all students learn at the same speed. Some need more time for certain topics. Our home schooling model allows students to move forward only after they fully understand the concept.

Concept-Focused Approach

We focus on building strong basics. Instead of memorizing answers, students are encouraged to understand the “why” behind each topic.

Continuous Doubt Support

Students can ask questions during live sessions and receive immediate clarification. This reduces confusion and builds confidence.

Assessments and Progress Tracking

Assessment in online home schooling should help students grow, not create unnecessary pressure. Our evaluation system focuses on understanding and progress.

How We Assess Students

●  Topic-based tests

●  Flexible assessment schedules

●  Continuous monitoring

●  Topics covered

●  Strength areas

This helps parents stay informed and involved in the learning process.

Certification and Academic Records

At the end of the academic cycle, students receive:

Role of Parents in Home Schooling

Home schooling works best when parents stay actively involved. We encourage parents to be part of the academic journey.

●  Monitoring academic reports

Our educators regularly communicate with parents to maintain alignment and ensure steady progress.
