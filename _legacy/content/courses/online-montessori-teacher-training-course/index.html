What is Ilming’s ECPC Course?

The Early Childhood Preparatory Course (ECPC) is designed to help individuals understand how to teach and care for young children. It covers child development, effective learning methods, and ways to create a supportive, engaging environment for kids. Through this course, your role will be to gain a strong foundation in Early Childhood Education (ECE) and more, preparing you to nurture young learners with the right balance of knowledge, care, and creativity.

 

 

Just like the  Montessori approach, ECPC encourages hands-on learning, where children explore and learn at their own pace. Teachers or caregivers enrolled in a Montessori training course are trained to guide children gently, providing them with the right tools and activities to support their growth.

Ilming's ECPC Course Curriculum: A Complete Montessori Training Experience

Our Montessori teacher training program is divided into two main components:

1. Theory Modules

  1.  Montessori Teaching Methods: Learn different teaching philosophies and how Montessori differs from traditional education.
  2.  School Management & Administration: Understand classroom organization, daily planning, and school operations.
  3.  Child Psychology: Study child development, emotional needs, and learning patterns
  4.  Health & Nutrition: Learn about children’s diet, hygiene, first aid, and common childhood illnesses.
  5.  Communicative English: Improve your language and communication skills for effective teaching

2. Practical Training

  1. Practical Life Exercises (EPL) Zone: Develop independence and coordination skills in children.
  2. Sensorial Zone: Strengthen children’s senses through hands-on learning experiences
  3. Language Zone: Teach listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills using Montessori techniques
  4. Arithmetic & Logic Zone: Introduce math concepts through engaging and fun activities
