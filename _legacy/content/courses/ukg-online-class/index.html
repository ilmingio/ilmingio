UKG Online Class

Strengthen your child’s foundation with our online UKG classes that focus on advanced reading, writing, math, and real-world understanding through fun, hands-on learning. Designed for children aged 4.5 to 5.5 years, our NEP 2020-aligned program prepares them confidently for Class 1.

Build Confidence and Skills Before Primary School Starts with an Online UKG Class

Ilming Learning’s Online UKG Classes are designed to prepare children for primary school through fun, interactive, and activity-based learning. 

Aimed at children aged 4.5 to 5.5 years, this program focuses on reading, writing, numbers, general awareness, creativity, and social development.

Every lesson is taught by experienced early childhood educators using a mix of live classes, printable worksheets, stories, and hands-on activities. This helps children build strong foundations without stress.

 

 

What is UKG?

UKG stands for Upper Kindergarten. It is the second and final year of the kindergarten stage, typically for children between 4.5 and 5.5 years old. 

After completing LKG (Lower Kindergarten), UKG helps children become school-ready by strengthening their basic reading, writing, math, and social skills in a structured but playful way.

 

UKG Online Classes at Ilming Learning

Our UKG classes follow a well-rounded curriculum aligned with CBSE, ICSE, and State syllabuses. The program is fully online, designed to combine screen-time learning with offline worksheets and creative play.

 

Subjects and Topics Covered

●   English Language: Three- and four-letter words, simple sentences, phonics, cursive writing, vocabulary, storytelling, picture reading, rhymes, and communication.
●   Mathematics: Numbers 1 to 100, backward counting, before/after, greater/lesser, addition, subtraction, shapes, patterns, sequencing, number names.

●   Environmental Science (EVS) and General Awareness: Myself, my family, community helpers, seasons, plants, animals, safety, hygiene, and our environment.

●   Hindi: Hindi alphabets (vowels and consonants), rhymes, daily vocabulary, sentence formation, and listening activities.

●   Creative Arts & Craft: Drawing, coloring, cutting, pasting, and paper folding—helping children express themselves.

 

How We Teach in UKG

Learning Goals for UKG Students

●   Read and write simple words and sentences

●   Count, add, and subtract numbers up to 100

●   Understand the world around them—family, nature, safety, and hygiene

●   Express ideas through speaking, drawing, movement, and music

●   Follow routines, develop patience, and interact well with others

●   Prepare confidently for entry into Class 1.
