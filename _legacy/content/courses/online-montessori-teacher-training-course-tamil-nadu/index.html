What is ECPC?

ECPC stands for Early Childhood Preparatory Course. It is designed to prepare educators and caregivers to understand young children’s needs, learning styles, and developmental stages.

Through ECPC, you learn:

● Basics of child psychology and development.

● How to design activities that encourage curiosity and creativity.

● Effective classroom management skills.

● How to create a safe, engaging, and supportive learning environment.

It builds the foundation for a strong career in early childhood education and is closely aligned with Montessori principles.

Montessori Training Methodology in TN

The Montessori approach is what makes this course so unique. Here are its core principles:

● Child-Centered Learning: Children choose activities based on their interests, fostering independence.

● Hands-On Activities: Real materials and practical exercises make concepts easier to understand.

● Prepared Environment: Classrooms are set up in a way that encourages self-learning and exploration.

● Whole Child Development: Focuses equally on academic, emotional, social, and physical growth.

This methodology makes Montessori teachers highly valued, as they bring in skills beyond traditional teaching.
