What Is Montessori Training?

Montessori training is a structured program that teaches you how to work with young children using Dr. Maria Montessori’s child-centered method. The training covers Montessori theory, hands-on material usage, lesson presentations, child psychology, observation techniques, and classroom management.

The goal is to prepare teachers who can:

● Guide children in a mixed-age environment

● Support independence through purposeful activities

● Present materials scientifically and respectfully

● Observe children’s needs and learning patterns

● Build a calm, engaging, and self-paced classroom

This training prepares you for Montessori schools, preschools, daycare centers, and early learning programs across Kerala.

Why Montessori Training Is in Demand in Kerala

Kerala’s strong literacy background and rising number of preschools have created continuous demand for trained early childhood educators. Parents are now more aware of activity-based learning, motor-skill development, and foundational education quality.

Key reasons for growing demand:

● A sharp increase in private preschools across cities and towns

● NEP-aligned focus on ages 3–8

● Lack of trained Montessori teachers in many districts

● Rising demand for preschool teachers who understand early childhood psychology

● More housewives and mothers are seeking flexible, home-friendly careers

As a result, Montessori teacher training course in Kerala has become a popular choice for beginners and experienced teachers.

Why Montessori Training Is Becoming Highly Relevant in Kerala

Over the past few years, there has been a rapid growth in private preschools, daycares, and early-learning centres across Kerala’s towns and cities. Demand for child-centred, activity-based learning is increasing as parents become more aware of foundational education. 

Many rural and semi-urban areas — traditionally underserved — now have Montessori or preschool setups. However, there’s a shortage of properly trained Montessori teachers relative to the number of schools operating. 

Families are increasingly looking for quality early childhood education (ECE), and schools are actively seeking certified Montessori educators. That makes this the right time to train and enter the field. 

Because of this growing demand, a trained Montessori teacher from Kerala today has many more options than ever — from local preschools to home-based daycare setups, or even opportunities abroad (Gulf or overseas international schools).

What Is Ilming’s ECPC Course?

Ilming’s Early Childhood Preparatory Course (ECPC) helps you learn how to support and teach young children in their early years. The course explains how children grow, how they learn best, and how to create a positive and engaging classroom atmosphere. It builds your basic understanding of Early Childhood Education (ECE) and teaches you the practical skills needed to guide young learners with confidence, patience, and creativity.

What You will Learn in this Montessori Teacher Training Course

Full Montessori Curriculum + Child Development + Practical Exposure

Our course covers everything needed to become a confident Montessori educator:

● Montessori Philosophy & Child-Centred Education: Understanding the absorbent mind, sensitive periods, and the role of a Montessori teacher.

● Practical Life Activities: Care of self and environment, motor-skill development, life skills.

● Sensorial Activities: Using Montessori material to develop senses (shape, size, weight, color, etc.).

● Language Readiness: Phonetic teaching, sandpaper letters, moveable alphabet, preparing children for reading and writing.

● Mathematics Foundation: Concrete math concepts using Montessori materials — number rods, golden bead material, quantity-symbol matching.

● Child Psychology & Behaviour Observation: Understanding developmental milestones, observing behaviour, communicating positively.

● Classroom Management & Mixed-Age Handling: Setting up prepared environments, organising activities, documenting progress.

● Practical Assignments + Lesson Demonstration Videos: To give you hands-on familiarity even before the internship.

● Internship & Practicum Support in Kerala Preschools: Guided observation hours, real-classroom practice, material usage, lesson delivery, and documentation.

● Placement Assistance & Career Support: Resume building, interview preparation, and preschool referrals across Kerala districts.

The course is delivered online (live + recorded), with both Malayalam- and English-medium tracks — making it highly accessible to learners across Kerala, whether urban or rural.
