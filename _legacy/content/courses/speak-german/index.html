Course Structure – From A1 to B1

Our course follows a progressive structure across four main modules:

Module 1: Basics & Foundation (A1)

  1. Alphabet & Pronunciation
  2. Basic Phrases
  3. Number System
  4. Grammar Introduction
  5. Self-Introduction

Module 2: Communication & Grammar (A1–A2)

  1. Personal Information
  2. Family & Relationships
  3. Cafe & Restaurant Conversations
  4. Shopping & Quantities

  1. Around the City
  2. Health & Doctor Visits
  3. Home & Living
  4. Hobbies & Free Time
  5. Weather & Seasons

Module 4: Advanced Applications (B1 Topics)

  1. Travel & Transportation
  2. Jobs & Work Life
  3. Festivals & Traditions
  4. Opinions & Preferences
  5. Review & Real-Life Conversations

How Long Does It Take To Learn German?

Each CEFR level (A1, A2, B1) includes approximately 50 sessions. Your total duration may vary based on frequency, dedication, and learning style. With consistent effort, you can reach B1 in 4–6 months.

With regular practice, you can go from A1 to B2 in about 9 to 12 months. It may vary, but the journey gets easier when you know what to expect.
