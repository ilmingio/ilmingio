Exams Security

Secure online exams for Islamic schools

IL ilming Team 8 May 2026 6 min read

Online exams for Islamic studies, Arabic, and tajweed assessments need the same integrity standards as academic exams — but many platforms either over-promise AI surveillance or offer no controls at all.

Controls that actually help

ilming provides per-exam settings your staff can turn on or off:

  • Fullscreen mode — reduces casual tab switching
  • Tab-switch detection — logs when students leave the exam window
  • Copy/paste blocking — limits answer sharing during the attempt
  • Webcam presence check — optional presence verification with violation logs

What we don't claim

ilming does not use AI face recognition or continuous screen recording. Those approaches raise privacy concerns and often fail in real madrassa and home environments. Instead, we give institutes clear violation logs to review after each exam.

Integrity tools should support your staff's judgement — not replace it with black-box AI.

Question banks and Islamic content

Build banks for Qur'an memorisation tests, Islamic history, fiqh modules, or combined secular subjects. Shuffle questions, set time limits, and publish results when your admin team is ready.
