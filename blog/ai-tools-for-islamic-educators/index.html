AI tools for Islamic educators — used responsibly

IL ilming Team 28 April 2026 5 min read

AI in education is often marketed as a replacement for teachers. In Islamic institutes, that framing misses the point — and creates legitimate concern among scholars, parents, and Ustadh.

AI as a prep tool, not a teacher

ilming's optional AI features (beta) are designed for staff efficiency:

  • Draft exam questions from topics you specify
  • Outline term plans aligned to your syllabus
  • Generate practice quizzes for revision weeks
  • Help students understand mistakes after graded attempts

Every exam question still goes through human review before students see it. Your institute controls whether AI is enabled at all.

Scoped to your materials

Student assistants work within course content you upload — not open-ended web search. That keeps answers closer to what your madrassa or school actually teaches.

AI should save prep time for Ustadh — not publish content without their approval.

When to skip AI entirely

Many institutes run ilming without AI and get full value from exams, fees, guardians, and live classes. AI is an add-on for teams ready to experiment — not a requirement.
